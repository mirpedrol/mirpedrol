### Hi! 👋

I'm Júlia. I write code to help bioinformaticians!

### My story

I have a background in Biology, with a focus on Genetics and Genomics. During my undergrad, I discovered I could combine two things I love, understanding how genomes work and creating through code, through bioinformatics.

Although I’m still passionate about genetics and synthetic biology, I’ve shifted toward a more technical path. 


I code mostly in Python. <img src="https://img.icons8.com/color/48/python--v1.png" width="3.5%"/>
During my master’s thesis I fell in love with Nextflow. <img src="https://github.com/nextflow-io/trademark/blob/master/nextflow-icon.png?raw=true" width="3.5%"/>

### What I do

I’m an active supporter of open source, part of the [nf-core](https://nf-co.re/) <img src="https://github.com/nf-core/logos/blob/master/nf-core-logos/nf-core-logo-square.png?raw=true" width="3.5%"/> community and a Nextflow ambassador.

I’m also committed to supporting women in STEM: I’ve been part of [Young IT Girls](https://youngitgirls.org/) (check them out, they are cool!) and a mentor in the [Technovation Challenge](https://technovationchallenge.org/es/). I love helping young girls and women grow in tech.

I am a researcher. Currently working at the CRG, developing a way to allow the benchmark of analysis tools.
Recently, I’ve been diving into LLMs and MCP, and I really enjoy learning new things.

I’m also a freelancer. I have started working on a personal project to help coding teachers guide their students better. And also open to small projects and collaborations.
